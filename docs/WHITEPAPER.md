# GRM Mining — Project Whitepaper (Public Information Draft)

**Document status:** Public project-information draft. This document is not an audit, legal opinion, investment offer, or certification.

## 1. Project overview

GRM Mining is a Telegram-based rewards project. Users may earn GRM rewards through hourly mining, approved tasks, daily check-ins, and referral rewards, subject to the available reward pool and the project's operating rules.

- **Reward token symbol:** GRM
- **Configured network:** TON
- **Telegram bot:** https://t.me/GRM_Mining_Reward_Bot
- **Mining/reward pool allocation:** 172,000,000 GRM

The 172,000,000 GRM figure describes the allocation reserved for mining and rewards in the bot. It must not be interpreted as the total token supply.

## 2. Contract information

The official GRM contract address is currently under verification and is intentionally not published here. It will be added after the project team confirms the correct address.

**Company wallet for the activation payment:**

`UQBGbzaKDO0j_gddN5qguj15BrI6L9_VGcnUT0xP6rGXK7KT`

The company activation wallet is not the GRM token contract address. Users should not confuse the two.

## 3. Mandatory account activation

Before mining or using reward features, each user must:

1. Register their own TON wallet in the bot using `/setwallet WALLET_ADDRESS`.
2. Send **0.1 GRAM** to the company activation wallet shown above.
3. Submit the transaction hash using `/activate TX_HASH`.
4. Wait for an administrator to check and confirm the payment.

Mining and reward features remain unavailable until the administrator confirms activation. In the current bot version, this payment review is manual; the bot does not independently verify blockchain transactions.

## 4. Daily check-in rewards

Users must complete **30 successful daily check-ins** to satisfy the check-in requirement for withdrawal.

- Check-in 1: 50 GRM.
- The reward increases by 50 GRM for each successful check-in.
- Check-in 30: 1,500 GRM.
- Total scheduled reward for all 30 successful check-ins: 23,250 GRM.

A user can check in once per calendar day. If a calendar day is missed, progress pauses rather than resetting. The next successful check-in continues with the next check-in number. The user does not receive a reward for a missed day.

## 5. Referrals

Each user can share a personal referral link generated by the bot.

A referral is successful only when the invited person:
1. Joins using the inviter's referral link.
2. Registers their wallet.
3. Pays 0.1 GRAM to the company activation wallet.
4. Has the activation payment confirmed by an administrator.

After that confirmation, the inviter receives **500 GRM** for that successful referral. The reward is credited once per invited account and is deducted from the bot's reward pool.

## 6. Withdrawals

Withdrawal requests are subject to all of the following:

- At least **30 successful daily check-ins**.
- A minimum withdrawal request of **17,000 GRM**.
- A **10% withdrawal fee**.
- Administrator review and approval.

If approved, the intended net amount is 90% of the requested amount. In the current bot version, the administrator handles the actual blockchain payout manually and records its transaction hash. The bot does not automatically send tokens on-chain.

## 7. Tasks, trades and lottery

- Task rewards require evidence submission and administrator review.
- Trade requests are submitted for administrator review; approval in the bot is not itself an on-chain settlement.
- Lottery participation and prize handling follow the specific lottery configured by the administrator.

## 8. Reward pool and limitations

The configured mining/reward pool starts at **172,000,000 GRM**. Hourly mining, task rewards, check-in rewards, referral rewards, and lottery prizes are subject to the remaining pool and the bot's rules.

The following details have not been supplied or independently established in this document: total token supply, token decimals, independent smart-contract audit, legal or regulatory certification, and any guarantee of token market value or liquidity. No such claims are made.

## 9. Security

- Never share your wallet seed phrase, recovery phrase, or private key with anyone, including project administrators.
- Only send the activation fee to the company wallet displayed in the official bot.
- Carefully check the wallet address and transaction details before sending.
- Do not treat internal bot balances as proof of an on-chain token transfer.
