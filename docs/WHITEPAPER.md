## Overview
EmpireCoin is a community-driven digital token. The planned Telegram rewards bot is intended to support community engagement, task-based participation, referrals, and reward accounting.

## Planned bot functions
- Hourly reward claims (subject to limits and rules)
- Fixed daily check-in reward: 100 EMPC per successful check-in (3,000 EMPC across 30 successful check-ins)
- Task submission with administrator review
- Referral tracking
- Internal balance and transaction history
- Wallet registration and withdrawal-request review
- Telegram Mini App dashboard

These features are planned or under rebuild and should not be considered live or guaranteed.

## Important limitations and disclosures
- Internal bot balances are not on-chain token holdings.
- The bot does not currently automate or guarantee blockchain payouts.
- Token value, liquidity, rewards, or profits are not guaranteed.
- The bot is not audited; test thoroughly before launch.
- No activation payments will be solicited during the rebuild/testing stage.
- Users must never share recovery phrases or private keys.
